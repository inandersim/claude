The '''Everest Base Camp trek''' is one of the most popular treks in [[Nepal]], leading to the foot of the world's highest mountain.<ref>Nepal Tourism Board</ref>

==Understand==
The trek follows the Dudh Kosi valley through the Khumbu region. Most trekkers take 12–14 days.

===History===
The route was first used by the 1953 expedition.

==Get in==
* Fly from [[Kathmandu]] to [[Lukla]] (30 min). During peak season flights leave from Ramechhap.
* Alternatively take a jeep to Salleri and walk two extra days.

==Fees and permits==
{{listing|name=Sagarmatha National Park permit|price=NPR 3000|content=Bought in Kathmandu or Monjo.}}
* Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality permit: NPR 2000.

==Stay safe==
Altitude sickness is the main danger. Ascend slowly and take rest days at Namche and Dingboche.
Helicopter evacuation requires insurance.
